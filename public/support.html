How do I add an expense?

Tap the + button at the bottom of the screen, enter the amount, choose a category, and tap "Add expense". It takes seconds.

How do I add a subscription?

Open the Subscriptions tab, tap "Add subscription", and enter the service name, amount, billing frequency, and next payment date.

Can I use multiple currencies?

Flow works with one currency at a time — EUR, USD, GBP, CHF, CAD, AUD, or ILS. Change it any time in Settings → Preferences.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. Settings → Data → Delete all data removes everything from this device. It cannot be undone.

Does the app connect to my bank?

No. Flow is manual and local-first by design. Your records are entered by you, and there is no bank connection or automatic import.

Does the app sell my data?

No. There is no account, no server, and no advertising. Your financial data never leaves your device unless you export or share it yourself.

Does Flow cancel subscriptions?

No. Flow only tracks them. Canceling a subscription record in the app does not affect the real-world subscription — you'd still need to cancel it with the provider.
