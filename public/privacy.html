Privacy Policy

Flow is built around one idea: your money is your business. That's why the app has no accounts, no servers, and no tracking.

What we store

Your transactions, subscriptions, budgets, categories, and preferences. All of it is stored locally on your device in your browser's built-in storage (IndexedDB).

Where it's stored

On your device only. There is no Flow server, database, or cloud account. Your data is not synced anywhere.

Does anything leave the device?

Nothing is sent to us automatically. The only times data leaves your device are when you choose to do something with it: you export a CSV file and share or save it somewhere, or you enable notifications — your device's operating system handles delivery, and Flow does not see the content.

Analytics and crash reporting

Flow does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or any third-party services. There is nothing to disable because there is nothing collecting data.

Deleting your data

Go to Settings → Data → Delete all data. This erases everything stored by the app on this device. You can also clear your browser's storage for this site, or delete the app, at any time.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will appear here and in the app.
