Flow is a personal finance app that keeps every number on your device — no account, no server, no data collection.

TRACK SPENDING
Log expenses and income in seconds. Organize everything by category, search and filter your history, and see exactly where your money goes.

CONTROL SUBSCRIPTIONS
Keep every recurring subscription in one place. See what's coming up, get a reminder before each payment, and record payments with one tap.

STAY ON BUDGET
Set an overall monthly budget or budgets per category. Flow tracks how close you are and alerts you before you go over.

SEE THE BIG PICTURE
Monthly and yearly insights show your spending trends by category, so patterns are easy to spot.

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
Everything is stored privately on your device. Flow makes no network requests and has no backend — your financial information never leaves your phone. Export a full CSV backup any time, right from Settings.

Flow only tracks the subscriptions you tell it about — it never connects to your bank or cancels a real-world subscription on your behalf.
