**Last updated: August 25, 2026**

Flow ("the app") is developed by Actually Works ("we", "us"). This policy explains how Flow handles your information.

## The short version

Flow does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. Everything you enter — transactions, subscriptions, budgets, and settings — is stored only on your device. Flow has no server and makes no network requests.

## What Flow stores, and where

Flow stores the following types of information, all locally on your device (using iOS's on-device storage, never a remote server):

- Transactions you add (amount, category, merchant, date, notes)
- Subscriptions you track (service name, amount, billing frequency, category, payment history)
- Budgets you set
- App preferences (currency, theme, date format, notification settings)

None of this data is sent to us, to any third party, or to any analytics or advertising service, because Flow does not contain any such integration.

## Notifications

If you enable payment reminders or budget alerts, Flow schedules local notifications directly on your device using iOS's notification system. This does not involve any server and no notification content is sent anywhere.

## Data export

Flow lets you export your data as a CSV file at any time from Settings. This file is created and shared entirely through iOS's own share sheet (e.g., to save to Files, or to send via Mail or Messages) — Flow itself never uploads it anywhere.

## Data deletion

You can delete all of your data at any time from Settings ("Delete all data"), or by deleting the app, which removes everything Flow stored on your device.

## Children's privacy

Flow does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.
